## Additional proof-text correction found during the audit

In the proof of `lem:sec32_point_boundary`, the base case says that the resistance between the two terminals of a level-m cell **equals** resistance-rate^-m times its colour amplitude. For the defined global free-pair resistance R_*, other cells can provide parallel routes. The justified statement is **at most** the isolated-cell resistance. For example, in DHL(2,2) at the first level, adjacent endpoints in the four-cycle have unit effective resistance 3/4, whereas the individual edge has resistance one. The required base estimate A0 ≤ max amplitude remains valid with the inequality, by Rayleigh monotonicity. This is a local proof-text error, not a counterexample to the lemma.
